Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Summary: We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on new hosting and cloud plans. Refunds are not available for domain registrations, one-time setup fees, or add-on services used.

1. Eligibility for Refund

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the initial payment date.
  • Eligible services: Web Hosting (Starter, Business, Pro), VPS / Cloud plans (first month only).
  • Refunds apply only to new customers on their first subscription, not renewals or upgrades.

2. Non-Refundable Items

  • Domain name registrations and renewals (once registered, the cost is non-recoverable).
  • One-time setup fees, migration fees, and professional services.
  • SSL certificates already issued.
  • Add-on services that have been provisioned and used (e.g., CDN, monitoring).
  • Services cancelled after 7 days of purchase.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated due to violations of our Terms of Service.

3. Refund Process

  • Submit your refund request to support@apexcloudit.in with your order ID and reason for refund.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of approval.
  • Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method (UPI, card, or bank account).
  • Bank processing time may add an additional 3–5 business days.

4. Service Credits

In cases of prolonged unscheduled downtime exceeding our 99.9% SLA, we may issue service credits to your account for the affected period. Credits cannot be converted to cash.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email us at support@apexcloudit.in with the subject line "Refund Request – [Your Order ID]" or visit our Contact page.
