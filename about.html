About Apex Cloud & IT Services

We are an India-based technology company providing enterprise-grade web hosting, cloud infrastructure, and IT solutions to businesses since 2019.

Our Story

Powering Indian Businesses with Reliable IT

Apex Cloud & IT Services was founded in 2019 by ISHWAR PRASAD with a simple mission: to give Indian businesses access to enterprise-quality cloud infrastructure and IT services at fair, transparent prices.

Starting as a web hosting provider, we have grown into a full-service IT solutions company serving startups, SMEs, and enterprises across India. We manage hundreds of servers, support thousands of websites, and provide ongoing IT maintenance to organisations in multiple sectors.

Every service we offer is delivered by our in-house technical team. We do not resell third-party services without disclosing it. We believe in honest billing, responsive support, and long-term partnerships with our clients.
